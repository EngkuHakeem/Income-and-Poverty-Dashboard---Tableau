# Income-and-Poverty-Dashboard-DOSM 📊

## 📌 Overview
This project focuses on analyzing publicly available **income and poverty datasets from the Department of Statistics Malaysia (DOSM)** to understand regional income distribution and socio-economic indicators across Malaysia.

The objective of this project was to transform raw statistical datasets into **interactive dashboards and visualizations** that allow users to explore income trends, poverty levels, and regional disparities in a more intuitive way.

Using **Tableau and Microsoft Excel**, the datasets were cleaned, structured, and visualized to communicate insights through clear **data storytelling and information visualization**.

---

## 🎯 My Role

In this project,

- Identified relevant **income and poverty datasets from DOSM**
- Cleaned and prepared datasets using **Microsoft Excel**
- Conducted **exploratory data analysis**
- Defined key **socio-economic indicators for visualization**
- Designed and developed **interactive dashboards in Tableau**
- Structured visualizations to support **data storytelling**
- Presented insights on regional income and poverty trends

The goal was to make complex statistical datasets easier to understand through **clear visualizations and dashboards**.

---

## ⚠️ Problem

Public statistical datasets often present several challenges:

- ❌ Raw datasets are difficult for non-technical audiences to interpret
- 📊 Large tables of statistical data make trend analysis challenging
- 📉 Regional disparities are not clearly visualized
- 📂 Socio-economic indicators are distributed across multiple reports

Without proper visualization, it becomes difficult to extract **meaningful insights from national statistics**.

---

## 💡 Solution

To address these challenges, I developed **interactive dashboards using Tableau** to present income and poverty indicators visually.

The dashboards allow users to explore trends and patterns through visualizations that support **data-driven storytelling**.

### Dashboard Features

- Regional income distribution across Malaysian states
- Poverty rate comparison between regions
- Income trend analysis
- Socio-economic indicators visualized through charts and maps
- Interactive filters for deeper analysis

The dashboard was designed to **simplify interpretation of complex statistical datasets**.

---
